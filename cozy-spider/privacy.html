Privacy Policy

Cozy Spider Solitaire · Last updated: July 2, 2026

Cozy Spider Solitaire (the “App”) is an offline, single-player card game. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what the App does — and does not — do with your information.

Information we collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account, no login, and no sign-up.

Data stored on your device

The App saves the following only on your device, using the system’s local storage, so your game works and remembers your progress:

  • Game settings (sound, music, haptics, language)
  • Statistics (games cleared, best score, best time)
  • In-progress saved games and your daily “garden” progress

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the App removes it.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The App contains no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites.

Third parties

The App does not include third-party services that collect your data.

Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
